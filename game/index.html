HOW TO PLAY

Two pictures of the same harbour sit side by side. The right one hides 8 changes. Find them all before the hearts run out.

Click a difference on either panel — left and right count the same. A right click rings it green on both panels. A wrong click costs one heart.

Finds in a row within 5 seconds raise your combo: ×2, ×3, up to ×4. A wrong click breaks it.

Finish under the par time with hearts to spare for 3 stars. A hint costs you a star.

Keyboard: arrows move the square cursor, Enter or Space clicks, H hints, P pauses, M magnifies.
